# Code of conduct

Participants must treat one another respectfully, discuss evidence and technical decisions without personal attacks, and protect private information. Harassment, threats, discrimination and publication of another person's confidential material are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to protoscience@anulum.li. Include the relevant context and the outcome sought; avoid distributing sensitive material beyond what the maintainer needs. The maintainer may request clarification, moderate discussion, restrict participation or remove material in proportion to the conduct. A person affected by a moderation decision may request reconsideration through the same private route.
